Glossary

指标说明与更新频率

本说明对着看板上的四张顶栏：上游/原料报价、加工/液化、跨大西洋航运、终端/供暖/柴油，以及下面的欧洲库存、美湾到西北欧套利和炼厂。先讲每个数在业务上是什么、在这页上怎么读。除欧元兑美元（EURUSD）外，价格、库存、运价和采暖度日（HDD）都是示意。改任何一个输入，图表和套利会立刻重算。

能量单位全篇只使用两种写法：价格和成本用美元/百万英热（MMBtu）或欧元/兆瓦时（MWh）；货量用百万英热（MMBtu）。货量输入框上的「万 MMBtu」与万百万英热（MMBtu）是同一个单位。

上游原料

Henry Hub

Henry Hub 是美国路易斯安那州的天然气现货枢纽，报价为美元/百万英热（MMBtu）。它是美湾液化天然气的原料价格。

不要把看板上的 Henry Hub 直接当成到岸气价。液化厂自己要烧掉一部分气，原料端先放大。

进料气（美元/百万英热（MMBtu））= Henry Hub × 1.15

1.15 是本页固定的进料气系数：每出口 1 百万英热（MMBtu）液化气，按多买 15% 原料气，用来覆盖厂内燃料和损耗。这不是 EIA 公布的系数。

WTI

WTI（西得克萨斯中质原油）是美国轻质低硫原油基准，定价地在俄克拉何马州库欣，单位美元/桶。它描述美国上游原油，也是内陆炼厂的原油成本。

库欣库存松、外输管道受限时，WTI 会相对海上原油走弱。本页没有 WTI 输入，柴油裂解也不减 WTI。

布伦特（Brent）

布伦特（Brent） 是本页使用的原油价格，单位美元/桶。纽约港、美湾和鹿特丹的成品油对照的是海上原油，不是库欣内陆价格。

裂解先把成品换成美元/桶，再减去这一个布伦特（Brent）。看板上的数字是示意起点，不是 EIA 当日现货。

加工/液化

美国炼厂开工率

开工率 是可运行炼油能力里、当周实际进料的百分比。

开工高，汽油和馏分油的供给偏松。开工下降，成品油更容易抽紧，柴油裂解往往走阔。这个读数只说明美国炼厂松紧，不进入套利，也不进入裂解。

液化进料气与液化费

进料气和液化费是两笔钱。

进料气 是按 Henry Hub 买来的原料，已经乘过 1.15，单位美元/百万英热（MMBtu）。液化费 是把气冻成液体的加工费，起点 2.50 美元/百万英热（MMBtu），可以改。

离岸（美元/百万英热（MMBtu））= 进料气 + 液化费 = Henry Hub × 1.15 + 液化费

EIA《天然气周报》里的液化进料气，是液化厂实际吃进的管道气，是实物量。本页的 1.15 只是燃料气放大系数，不要把它读成那条序列。交付成本在离岸之上再加运费、蒸发气、港口和再气化，见下一节。

海运与套利

Spark25 日租金

Spark25 是 Spark Commodities 对大西洋盆地液化天然气船日租金的评估，单位美元/日。日租金越高，同一条船的航次越贵，美湾到欧洲的窗口越容易关上。

现货评估 Spark25S 每个评估日发布：提交窗口为格林尼治时间 08:00–10:00，10:00 GMT 公布。远期 Spark25Fo 每周二发布一次。

曲线左段是示意轨迹，右端点跟随当前日租金，不是 Spark 的正式估价。

运费（美元/百万英热（MMBtu））= 日租金（美元/日）× 往返天数 / 货量（百万英热（MMBtu））

货量（百万英热（MMBtu））= 输入的万百万英热（MMBtu）× 10,000

起点是日租金 55,000 美元/日、往返 24 天、货量 360 万百万英热（MMBtu）。货量变小，或往返变长，单位运费上升。

交付成本与套利边际

TTF（荷兰所有权转让设施）是欧洲天然气枢纽价，本页用欧元/兆瓦时（MWh）。它表示这船货在欧洲能卖掉的价格。看板上的 TTF 是示意输入，不是 ICE 当日结算价。

比较必须先换成同一个能量单位。交付成本先在美元/百万英热（MMBtu）里加总，再换成欧元/兆瓦时（MWh）。

蒸发气（美元/百万英热（MMBtu））= 装载天数 × 0.10%/日 × 离岸

交付成本（美元/百万英热（MMBtu））= 离岸 + 运费 + 蒸发气 + 港口 0.12 + 再气化 0.35

交付成本（欧元/兆瓦时（MWh））= 交付成本（美元/百万英热（MMBtu））× 3.412141633 / EURUSD

套利边际（欧元/兆瓦时（MWh））= TTF − 交付成本（欧元/兆瓦时（MWh））

边际高于 0.50 欧元/兆瓦时（MWh）时，窗口视为开启

0.10%/日 就是 0.001。装载天数大于往返天数时，蒸发气会被高估，页面会提示。港口 0.12 与再气化 0.35 的单位都是美元/百万英热（MMBtu），是本页固定项。

3.412141633 是 1 兆瓦时（MWh）对应的百万英热（MMBtu）。EURUSD 是 1 欧元兑多少美元，与欧洲央行标价一致。USDEUR 是它的倒数。0.50 欧元/兆瓦时（MWh）是这块看板的示意门槛，不是交易所规则。

边际为正，表示欧洲气价盖过了整叠成本：买美国气、乘进料气系数、付液化费、跨大西洋船运，再加上蒸发气、港口和再气化。这时美湾货有往欧洲拉的激励。Henry Hub、液化费或 Spark25 日租金上升，都会抬高交付成本，把边际压小。

欧元兑美元（EURUSD）

欧元兑美元（EURUSD） 约每 60 秒向 Frankfurter 取一次欧洲央行参考价，单次等待 8 秒。取到之后，没有被锁定的汇率会立刻重算套利和柴油热值。

失败时沿用上次成功值。从未成功则用 1.1204。参考价在每个 TARGET 工作日发布，约欧洲中部时间 16:00。手动改过的汇率会锁定，直到点「采用参考价」。

终端需求

欧盟库存：80% 与本页的 90% 刻度

工作气量 把库存百分比换成十亿立方米（bcm）。采暖季按寒冷程度往下抽。3 月末还剩多少，分成宽裕、平衡、紧张或耗尽。

工作气量（十亿立方米（bcm））= 库存% / 100 × 104.5

较五年均值 = 库存% − 93.2 个百分点

情景抽取（十亿立方米（bcm））= 58 ×（1 + 采暖度日偏离）

3 月末库存 = 起点工作气量 − 情景抽取

宽裕 ≥ 30 十亿立方米（bcm），平衡 ≥ 15 十亿立方米（bcm），否则紧张；低于零记为耗尽

104.5、93.2% 和 58 都是本页示意口径，不是当天 AGSI 的技术能力，也不是官方五年曲线。

法规里的 80% 是 2022 年的首年填充目标。Regulation (EU) 2022/1032 规定：2022 年 11 月 1 日前至少达到工作气能力的 80%，2023 年起每年 11 月 1 日前至少 90%。延期后的 Regulation (EU) 2025/1733 仍以 90% 为年度目标，但改成每年 10 月 1 日至 12 月 1 日之间达成，并允许成员国在市场困难或技术约束下偏离。

本页计算不用 80%。仪表上的琥珀色刻度是示意的 11 月 1 日 90% 目标，进度 = 当前库存% / 90%。它和 2023 年以后的法定填充水平同一量级，但刻度两边的数字是示意起点，不是 AGSI 当日读数。

采暖度日（HDD）

采暖度日（HDD） 把冷多少收成一个数：日均气温低于基温 15.5°C 的度数累加。越冷，取暖用气越多。

10 月到 3 月的「常年」柱是西北欧示意气候态，不是气象机构或 Eurostat 的正式序列。

情景采暖度日（HDD）= 常年采暖度日（HDD）×（1 + 采暖度日偏离）

偏离滑块同时放大柱状图，以及上面的 58 十亿立方米（bcm）抽取。偏离为正，表示比常年更冷，3 月末库存更低。

柴油裂解与 PADD 1

柴油裂解 是成品油相对原油还剩多少美元/桶。本页的原油腿用布伦特（Brent）。裂解越宽，炼厂越有动力多产柴油。裂解收窄甚至为负，柴油相对原油偏便宜。

柴油裂解（美元/桶）= 成品（美元/桶）− 布伦特（Brent）（美元/桶）

美元/加仑 × 42 = 美元/桶

美元/吨 / 7.45 = 美元/桶

纽约港和美湾的报价是美元/加仑，鹿特丹是美元/吨。42 加仑/桶、7.45 桶/吨是本页换算，不是交易所结算价。

PADD 1 是美国东岸。东岸馏分油库存低，说明消费地缓冲薄。库存紧、裂解宽，是成品油偏紧。库存厚、裂解窄，是供给松出来了。

东岸滑块的当前值写在曲线右端点。全美馏分油总库存只随东岸相对起点的增减，一比一移动。

馏分油总库存（百万桶）= 121.6 +（当前 PADD 1 − 29.4）

东岸在这里被当成边际变化，不是 EIA 对五个 PADD 的加总。起点是全美 121.6 百万桶，PADD 1 为 29.4 百万桶。

气和柴油放在同一个能量单位上比较：

柴油热值（欧元/兆瓦时（MWh））=（美元/吨 ÷ EURUSD）÷ 11.83

11.83 兆瓦时（MWh）/吨对应柴油低位热值 42.6 MJ/kg。气价高于这个热值时，本页把气转油激励标为出现：用户更愿意烧油、少烧气。右侧把 Henry Hub、TTF、鹿特丹瓦斯油和纽约港取暖油画成同一欧元/兆瓦时横条。Henry Hub 按 3.412141633 换算，柴油按 11.83。

附录：开发与数据更新指南

这一节给维护数据的人。看看板不需要读它。

页面加载时请求同目录的 data.json。页眉「数据快照」写出文件里的 asOf（按 UTC 显示），以及浏览器取得该文件的时间。请求失败、超时，或必填字段对不上时，整份文件丢弃，改用页内回退，快照写成「内置回退」。滑块和价格输入仍在已经载入的底稿上立刻重算。欧元兑美元（EURUSD）的 Frankfurter 轮询不读这个文件。

替换整个 data.json 即可，不必改 HTML。index.html 与 transatlantic-energy-dashboard.html 保持相同。解析器不读取 schema、kind、description。现行文件仍写 schema 为 energy-dashboard.data.v1，kind 为 illustrative。

必填字段：

  • asOf：非空字符串，用 ISO-8601 时间。
  • seed：下面每个键都是有限数字，并且 eurusd > 0、cargoWan > 0。
    • ttf：欧元/兆瓦时（MWh）
    • hh：美元/百万英热（MMBtu）
    • eurusd：1 欧元兑多少美元，只在参考价尚未取到时用作回退
    • freight：美元/日
    • storagePct、hddDevPct、utilPct：百分比
    • padd1：百万桶
    • brent：美元/桶
    • nyh、usgc：美元/加仑
    • ara：美元/吨
    • roundTripDays、ladenDays：天
    • cargoWan：万百万英热（MMBtu）
    • liq：美元/百万英热（MMBtu）
  • series.hddNorm：至少 1 个月。每项为 month 与 degreeDays，或 [月份, 度日]。
  • series.gasPremiumEurPerMwh 与 series.dieselPremiumEurPerMwh：欧元/兆瓦时（MWh），长度与 series.hddNorm 相同。
  • series.spark25UsdPerDay：美元/日，至少 2 个点。
  • series.padd1DistillateMillionBbl：百万桶，至少 2 个点。
  • inventory.usDistillateBaseMillionBbl 与 inventory.padd1BaseMillionBbl：文件里可以有，页面不读取。全美馏分油固定为 121.6 +（当前 PADD 1 − 29.4）。

曲线右端点会被当前输入盖住。因此 series.spark25UsdPerDay 的最后一个点应等于 seed.freight，series.padd1DistillateMillionBbl 的最后一个点应等于 seed.padd1。

下面这些系数留在页面里，替换 data.json 不会改它们：进料气 1.15；蒸发气 0.10%/日；港口 0.12 美元/百万英热（MMBtu）；再气化 0.35 美元/百万英热（MMBtu）；3.412141633 百万英热（MMBtu）/兆瓦时（MWh）；窗口门槛 0.50 欧元/兆瓦时（MWh）；工作气能力 104.5 十亿立方米（bcm）；五年均值 93.2%；11 月 1 日刻度 90%；常年抽取 58 十亿立方米（bcm）；42 加仑/桶；7.45 桶/吨；11.83 兆瓦时（MWh）/吨。

This note follows the four strips on the desk: feedstock quotes, liquefaction, transatlantic shipping, and end use (heating and diesel), then European storage, US Gulf to Northwest Europe arbitrage, and refining. It says what each number means and how to read it here. Except EURUSD, prices, stocks, freight, and heating degree days are illustrative. Change any input and the charts and the arb recalculate immediately.

Energy units stay in two forms: prices and costs in $/MMBtu or €/MWh, cargo in MMBtu. The cargo box labeled in 万 MMBtu is the same unit as MMBtu × 10,000.

Feedstock

Henry Hub

Henry Hub is the Louisiana natural-gas spot hub, quoted in $/MMBtu. It is the feedstock price for US Gulf LNG.

Do not read the Henry Hub print as a delivered gas price. The plant burns some gas, so the feedstock leg is grossed up first.

Feedgas ($/MMBtu) = Henry Hub × 1.15

1.15 is this page’s fixed feedgas factor: for each MMBtu exported, buy 15% more feedstock to cover plant fuel and loss. It is not an EIA coefficient.

WTI

WTI is the US light sweet crude benchmark, priced at Cushing in $/bbl. It describes inland US crude and inland refinery feedstock. This page has no WTI input, and diesel cracks do not subtract WTI.

Brent

Brent is the crude price used here, in $/bbl. New York Harbor, the US Gulf, and Rotterdam products are compared with seaborne crude, not Cushing. The crack converts the product to $/bbl, then subtracts this Brent. The desk number is an illustrative start, not the EIA print.

Processing and liquefaction

US refinery utilization

Utilization is the share of operable crude capacity that was charged that week. High runs loosen gasoline and distillate. Lower runs tighten products and often widen the diesel crack. The reading describes US refineries only. It does not enter the arb or the crack.

Feedgas and the liquefaction fee

Feedgas and the liquefaction fee are two charges. Feedgas is Henry Hub already multiplied by 1.15, in $/MMBtu. The liquefaction fee is the cost of freezing the gas, starting at 2.50 $/MMBtu, and it can be edited.

FOB ($/MMBtu) = feedgas + liquefaction fee = Henry Hub × 1.15 + fee

EIA feedgas is the physical pipeline gas a plant takes in. The 1.15 here is only a fuel-gas gross-up. Do not read it as that series. Delivered cost then adds freight, boil-off, port, and regas.

Shipping and arbitrage

Spark25 day rate

Spark25 is Spark Commodities’ assessment of Atlantic-basin LNG carrier day rates, in $/day. A higher day rate makes the same voyage dearer and shuts the US Gulf–Europe window more easily.

Spot Spark25S is published each assessment day: the window is 08:00–10:00 GMT and the print is at 10:00 GMT. Forward Spark25Fo is published on Tuesdays. The left side of the curve is illustrative. The right-hand point follows the current day rate. It is not a formal Spark assessment.

Freight ($/MMBtu) = day rate ($/day) × round-trip days / cargo (MMBtu)

Cargo (MMBtu) = the 万 MMBtu input × 10,000

The start is a 55,000 $/day rate, a 24-day round trip, and 360 万 MMBtu. A smaller cargo or a longer round trip raises unit freight.

Delivered cost and the arb margin

TTF is the European hub price, in €/MWh here. It is what the cargo can sell for in Europe. The desk TTF is an illustrative input, not the ICE settlement. Delivered cost is summed in $/MMBtu, then converted to €/MWh.

Boil-off ($/MMBtu) = laden days × 0.10%/day × FOB

Delivered ($/MMBtu) = FOB + freight + boil-off + port 0.12 + regas 0.35

Delivered (€/MWh) = delivered ($/MMBtu) × 3.412141633 / EURUSD

Arb margin (€/MWh) = TTF − delivered (€/MWh)

The window is open only when the margin is above 0.50 €/MWh

0.10%/day is 0.001. If laden days exceed the round trip, boil-off is overstated and the page says so. Port 0.12 and regas 0.35 are fixed $/MMBtu adders. 3.412141633 is MMBtu per MWh. EURUSD is dollars per euro, the ECB quoting convention. USDEUR is its reciprocal. 0.50 €/MWh is this desk’s illustrative threshold, not an exchange rule.

A positive margin means the European gas price covers the stack: US gas, the feedgas factor, the liquefaction fee, the Atlantic voyage, boil-off, port, and regas. US Gulf cargo then has a reason to point at Europe. A higher Henry Hub, liquefaction fee, or Spark25 day rate lifts delivered cost and compresses the margin.

EURUSD

EURUSD is polled from Frankfurter about every 60 seconds, with an 8-second timeout, for the ECB reference rate. After a print arrives, an unlocked rate recalculates the arb and the diesel heat value immediately.

A failure keeps the last good print. If none has ever arrived, the page uses 1.1204. The reference rate is published on TARGET business days, around 16:00 CET. A manual rate stays locked until “Use reference” is pressed.

End use

EU storage: the 80% rule and this page’s 90% tick

Working gas turns the stock percent into bcm. The winter draw depends on how cold it is. What is left at end-March is marked comfortable, balanced, tight, or exhausted.

Working gas (bcm) = stock% / 100 × 104.5

Versus the five-year average = stock% − 93.2 percentage points

Scenario withdrawal (bcm) = 58 × (1 + HDD deviation)

End-March stock = starting working gas − scenario withdrawal

Comfortable ≥ 30 bcm, balanced ≥ 15 bcm, otherwise tight; below zero is exhausted

104.5, 93.2%, and 58 are this page’s illustrative basis, not that day’s AGSI technical capacity and not an official five-year curve.

The legal 80% was the first-year fill target for 2022. Regulation (EU) 2022/1032 required at least 80% of working-gas capacity before 1 November 2022, and at least 90% before 1 November each year from 2023. The extended Regulation (EU) 2025/1733 still uses 90% as the annual target, but it must be met between 1 October and 1 December, and member states may deviate under market or technical stress.

This page does not use 80% in the math. The amber tick is an illustrative 1 November 90% target. Progress = current stock% / 90%. It is the same order of magnitude as the post-2023 legal fill, but the numbers on either side of the tick are an illustrative start, not the AGSI reading.

Heating degree days (HDD)

HDD compresses cold into one number: degrees by which the daily mean sits below a 15.5°C base, summed. Colder weather means more heating gas.

The October–March “normal” bars are an illustrative Northwest Europe climate, not a Eurostat or meteorological series.

Scenario HDD = normal HDD × (1 + HDD deviation)

The deviation slider scales both the columns and the 58 bcm withdrawal. A positive deviation is colder than normal, and end-March stocks finish lower.

Diesel cracks and PADD 1

The diesel crack is what is left of the product versus crude, in $/bbl. The crude leg is Brent. A wider crack gives refiners more reason to make diesel. A narrow or negative crack means diesel is cheap versus crude.

Diesel crack ($/bbl) = product ($/bbl) − Brent ($/bbl)

$/gallon × 42 = $/bbl

$/tonne / 7.45 = $/bbl

New York Harbor and the US Gulf are quoted in $/gallon. Rotterdam is $/tonne. 42 gallons per barrel and 7.45 barrels per tonne are this page’s conversions, not exchange settlements.

PADD 1 is the US East Coast. Low East Coast distillate stocks mean a thin buffer at the point of consumption. Tight stocks and a wide crack mean products are tight. Thick stocks and a narrow crack mean supply has loosened.

The slider’s current value is written on the right-hand point of the curve. US distillate stocks move one for one with the East Coast versus its start.

Distillate stocks (million bbl) = 121.6 + (current PADD 1 − 29.4)

The East Coast is treated as the marginal change, not as EIA’s sum of five PADDs. The start is 121.6 million bbl for the US and 29.4 million bbl for PADD 1.

Gas and diesel are compared on one energy unit:

Diesel heat (€/MWh) = ($/tonne ÷ EURUSD) ÷ 11.83

11.83 MWh per tonne matches a diesel lower heating value of 42.6 MJ/kg. When gas is above that heat value, this page marks a gas-to-oil incentive: users would rather burn oil. The bars on the right put Henry Hub, TTF, Rotterdam gasoil, and New York Harbor heating oil on one €/MWh scale. Henry Hub uses 3.412141633. Diesel uses 11.83.

Appendix: maintaining the data

This section is for the person who maintains the data. Reading the desk does not require it.

On load the page requests data.json in the same directory. The header snapshot prints the file’s asOf (shown in UTC) and the time the browser fetched the file. If the request fails, times out, or a required field does not match, the whole file is dropped and the inline fallback is used. The snapshot then says “Built-in fallback”. Sliders and price inputs still recalculate immediately on the loaded sheet. The Frankfurter EURUSD poll does not read this file.

Replace data.json as a whole. Do not edit the HTML. index.html and transatlantic-energy-dashboard.html stay identical. The parser does not read schema, kind, or description. The current file still sets schema to energy-dashboard.data.v1 and kind to illustrative.

Required fields:

  • asOf: a non-empty ISO-8601 string.
  • seed: every key below is a finite number, with eurusd > 0 and cargoWan > 0.
    • ttf: €/MWh
    • hh: $/MMBtu
    • eurusd: dollars per euro, used only before a reference rate arrives
    • freight: $/day
    • storagePct, hddDevPct, utilPct: percent
    • padd1: million bbl
    • brent: $/bbl
    • nyh, usgc: $/gallon
    • ara: $/tonne
    • roundTripDays, ladenDays: days
    • cargoWan: 万 MMBtu
    • liq: $/MMBtu
  • series.hddNorm: at least one month. Each item is month and degreeDays, or [month, degree days].
  • series.gasPremiumEurPerMwh and series.dieselPremiumEurPerMwh: €/MWh, same length as series.hddNorm.
  • series.spark25UsdPerDay: $/day, at least two points.
  • series.padd1DistillateMillionBbl: million bbl, at least two points.
  • inventory.usDistillateBaseMillionBbl and inventory.padd1BaseMillionBbl may be present. The page does not read them. US distillate stays 121.6 + (current PADD 1 − 29.4).

The current input overwrites the right-hand point. So the last series.spark25UsdPerDay point should equal seed.freight, and the last series.padd1DistillateMillionBbl point should equal seed.padd1.
